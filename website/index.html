01 / OVERVIEW

Built to move.
Designed to play.

CAT (Cellphone App-Controlled Toy) is a senior capstone project developed by three Electrical and Computer Engineering students at Oregon State University.

We're designing a spherical robot that can be controlled wirelessly through a smartphone application, combining embedded systems, custom electronics, and mechanical design.

Project stage: Preliminary design

02 / DESIGN DIRECTION

What we're building

01

Spherical motion

An enclosed robot with an internal mechanism for rolling movement.

02

Wireless control

Smartphone-based control through a wireless connection.

03

Custom electronics

Student-designed PCB subsystems and embedded control.

04

Interactive play

Exploring rechargeable power, wireless charging, and audio features.

Features shown are design goals, not completed or validated capabilities.
